# Default taste

Defaults learned from the review rounds of the first film made with this pipeline (a ski trip). People asked for
these, so they are the starting point. Any of them can be overridden per film in `event.yaml`; if the user's
own memory/preferences say otherwise, follow those.

## Picture
- **No still photos with Ken Burns zoom.** On 720p-era footage they looked "scuffed". Photos are for the review page.
- **No name cards** over people unless asked.
- Title: **kinetic and modern**: slam-in, flash, RGB split, wipe, glitch-out, on the beat. Never plain text
  over a video.
- Credits: film title + music only. No "filmed on GoPro and iPhones", no month/year line.
- Snow: muted, slightly cool grade; letterbox on scenic breathers only.

## Sound
- **The music never stops.** It ducks under voices (−6 dB) and sound bites (−14 dB) but never cuts to silence,
  and it lasts to the very end of the film.
- **Song changes happen under a talking clip**, with the new song creeping in quietly. Hard switches feel odd.
- **Camera sound only when someone talks, laughs or reacts.** Plain wind/ski/footstep noise stays muted.
- Funny sounds (singing, silly noises) work great as short punch-in sound bites with the music ducked.

## Content
- **Funny moments are the heart of it**: falls with laughter afterwards, singing, people messing about. Hunt
  for them in full-length clips, not only in action peaks. Keep 1-2 s after a fall for the reaction.
- Clips the user names (file + timestamp) are must-haves. If one can't be used, say so instead of dropping it.
- Cut boring stretches (empty pistes, repetitive POV) without asking.
- Slow motion only from real ≥ 50 fps sources (WhatsApp copies are sometimes 60 fps when album copies are 30).

## Helper pages
- Light theme; dark pages were hard to read.
- Naming people: one clear crop per unknown person, no duplicates, no group shots.

## Working style
- Offer concrete options with a recommendation, then get on with it.
- Feedback usually comes as preview timestamps; answer each point, then render a new preview.
